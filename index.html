FAQ

Common questions

Do you work only on Microsoft Power BI?

Mostly. We build on Power BI, Fabric, SQL, and Azure OpenAI in your Microsoft tenant. Excel cleanup is in scope when it feeds the model or the assistant.

Do you train an AI model on our data?

No. We use Microsoft's existing models through Fabric or Azure OpenAI. Your data is used to answer questions, not to train anything.

Where does our data go?

The assistant runs in your Microsoft tenant or Azure subscription wherever possible. You own the resources and pay Microsoft directly. Prompts and answers are not used to train Microsoft's or OpenAI's models.

Will it make up numbers?

Answers come from your governed sources and show the query or source document. Before go-live, we test real questions against answers you trust and give you the pass rate in writing.

Can people see data they shouldn't?

The assistant respects existing permissions. Power BI or SQL security continues to limit what each user can see. Access is read-only, with allowlisted views, row limits, and timeouts.

What do we need to have already?

Power BI Pro or data in SQL or Excel, plus a Microsoft 365 tenant. The in-Power BI option needs Fabric F2 capacity, about $160 to $265 per month from Microsoft. We confirm licensing on the intro call.

Our numbers are a mess. Is this for us?

Not yet. Start with the Reporting Health Check. If numbers do not reconcile, BI Jumpstart can fix the model before an assistant is added.

Who gets invoiced?

All work is invoiced by and paid to Nimblytica Inc (California S-corp). No W-2 and no contract-to-hire.

Can I buy the Kit without a consulting engagement?

Yes. The $149 Kit is a self-serve checklist. It does not include a live audit of your model.

How fast can work start?

After the intro call, a signed SOW, and the first invoice paid. Audit and alerts packs typically start within a few business days once access works.
