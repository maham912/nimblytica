FAQ

Common questions

Do you work only on Microsoft Power BI?

Yes. The core offers are Power BI semantic models, DAX, SQL sources, and Power Automate. Spreadsheet cleanup is in scope only when it feeds a Power BI build.

Who gets invoiced?

All work is invoiced by and paid to Nimblytica Inc (California S-corp). No W-2 and no contract-to-hire.

Do AI add-ons require Fabric Copilot capacity?

Alert Automation does not. The AI Weekly Brief defaults to Azure OpenAI in your subscription. Copilot is an option when you already have qualifying Fabric or Premium capacity.

Can I buy the Kit without a consulting engagement?

Yes. The $149 Kit is a self-serve checklist. It does not include a live audit of your model.

How fast can work start?

After the intro call, a signed SOW, and the first invoice paid. Audit and alerts packs typically start within a few business days once access works.
