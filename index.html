FAQ

Common questions

Do you work only on Microsoft Power BI?

Yes. The core offers are Power BI semantic models, DAX, SQL sources, and Power Automate. If your numbers live in Excel today, that is where we start; the fix usually ends in one trusted Power BI model.

Who gets invoiced?

All work is invoiced by and paid to Nimblytica Inc (California S-corp). No W-2 and no contract-to-hire.

What happens in the diagnostic?

20 minutes on a video call. You show us the report or spreadsheet people argue about and where the data comes from. We tell you which offer fits, or that you don't need one. You leave with a written next step. No slides, no obligation to buy.

Which offer do I start with?

Not sure where the numbers break: Reporting Health Check ($2.5k–$4k). You know the problem and need one trusted model and dashboards: BI Jumpstart ($10k–$18k, 3–5 weeks). It is built and you need someone to keep it right: Fractional BI ($3.5k–$6k/mo). Not sure? Take the 2-minute self-score.

Can I buy the Kit without a consulting engagement?

Yes. The $149 Kit is a self-serve checklist. It does not include a live audit of your model.

How fast can work start?

After the diagnostic, a signed SOW, and the first invoice paid. A Health Check typically starts within a few business days once access works.
