Do your numbers match? A 2-minute self-score

Eight yes or no questions. The recommendation is calculated in this browser from your answers only. Answers are not saved.

Questions

1. Do two reports ever show different numbers for the same thing (revenue, headcount, hours)?
2. Does someone rebuild the monthly numbers in Excel by hand?
3. Do leadership meetings spend time arguing about whose spreadsheet is right?
4. Do you pay for Power BI licenses that few people open?
5. Is there one person who is the only one who knows how the numbers are built?
6. Do definitions (what counts as a customer, an active employee, a closed deal) differ by team?
7. Do you already have a Power BI model or dashboards people mostly trust?
8. Do you need someone to keep reports right every month after they are fixed?

Result

Based only on your answers.

Scoring

How we score this

Checked in this order. The first rule that matches is the recommendation. Based only on your answers.

  1. 01

    Fractional BI ($3.5k–$6k/mo)

    If question 7 is yes and question 8 is yes: you already have a Power BI model or dashboards people mostly trust, and you need someone to keep reports right every month.

  2. 02

    BI Jumpstart ($10k–$18k)

    Otherwise, if question 7 is no and at least three of questions 1, 2, 3, 5, and 6 are yes: reports that disagree, monthly numbers rebuilt in Excel, meetings spent arguing about a spreadsheet, one person who knows how the numbers are built, or definitions that differ by team. Question 4 does not count toward those three.

  3. 03

    Reporting Health Check ($2.5k–$4k)

    Otherwise, if any of questions 1–6 is yes. Question 4 counts here. Yes answers on questions 1–6 still land here when question 7 is yes and question 8 is no, because Jumpstart requires question 7 to be no.

  4. 04

    You may not need us yet

    Otherwise: questions 1–6 are all no, and you did not answer yes to both question 7 and question 8. The $149 kit is a checklist you can run yourself.
