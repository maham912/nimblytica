The problem

Overtime hides in exports. By the time a spreadsheet is assembled, emailed, and screenshotted for the huddle, the numbers have moved and no one can slice them. Which unit is driving OT this week? Is it coverage or vacancies? The pack rarely answers.

What the board shows

  • Overtime hours by unit, trended over the last 12 months.
  • Headcount and span of control for the cut you pick.
  • Open roles and hiring pace against the gap OT is covering.
  • Attrition over the trailing year, so you can see churn behind the overtime.
  • The newest names on the unit, so the board matches who is actually on the floor.
