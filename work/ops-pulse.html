A daily pulse without the ticket dump

Case note. Open work, SLA, aging, and queue — the standing ops view. Public demo uses invented Lanternfield Service Ops data, not any live workbook.

Situation

Standups asked the same four questions: how much is open, what is late, what is aging, and which queue is holding it. The answers lived in a wide ticket extract. People scrolled rows instead of reading a pulse.

Constraint

The public page cannot carry ticket identifiers, people, or a real volume. The useful product is the cut: cards, a week trend, aging, queue. Row-level work stays off the site.

Build

  1. Overview cards. Open, SLA on time, breached still open, aging past eight days.
  2. Flow. Twelve weeks of opened and resolved on the same grain as the cards.
  3. Queue slicer. One control. Cards, charts, and the table follow.
  4. Aging and priority. Where work is stalling, and how sharp the mix is. No ticket list.

Result

The pulse is a short page, not an extract. The public version is the ops-pulse demo on round, invented numbers. Lanternfield Service Ops is a made-up employer on that file.
