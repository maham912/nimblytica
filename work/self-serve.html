Retire the ticketed pack

Case note. A ticketed pack retired in favor of a small self-serve snapshot. Public demo uses invented Northwind Health Ops data, not the original subject area.

Situation

Operations leads asked the same questions every cycle: what moved, what is concentrated, what is new. The answers lived in a file that one person refreshed and mailed. Queue time was the product.

Constraint

People did not need another 20-page workbook. They needed a short IA they could filter themselves, with an as-of date they could trust.

Build

  1. Overview cards. Headcount, open roles, attrition, span of control — four numbers, one cut.
  2. Trend. Twelve months on the same grain as the cards, so a spike is visible without a pivot.
  3. Department slicer. One control, every visual follows.
  4. Top and new lists. Where load sits, and who just started. Enough to decide the next check without exporting.

Result

The mailed pack retired. Managers opened the snapshot instead of waiting. The public version is the workforce demo on round, invented numbers. Alex Rivera is a made-up owner on that file.
